# Dim Ledger

Rolls: FORM=1 (trading run) SET=8 (pirate archipelago) ROLE=36 (prison warden) LOOP=13 (ration a shrinking supply) CLOCK=14 (morale, mutiny at zero) TWIST=43 (you are the villain of the story being told) WIN=17 (balance the budget, reviewer satisfied) MAP=13 (single room, named stations) TONE=4 (hard sci-fi) CURRENCY=6 (Light) TRACKED=2 (Light, Morale)

Premise: Warden of Skerry Nine, a debt-prison on a captured ice moonlet in a pirate archipelago. Kestrel Aune, Number 31, is writing a serial in which the Warden is the villain, and every chapter is a move against him. Light is the money, Morale is the stock; the Reviewer inspects the books after the last turn.

Rulesheet: run `python engine.py rules` (built from CONSTANTS).

Signature detail: the Chapel's rate is always 7 minus the Yard's. Kestrel's counter is market impact in disguise: a repeat of the same trade gets worse, a reversal at the same console gets better (ping-pong).

Midpoint plan: turn 8 the Pirate Lock opens (wide random rate, lot 4, out of Kestrel's reach); turn 11 the mirrors dim (burn 2 to 3). Late lever: from turn 14, once, doctor the ledger (Light -3, Reviewer roll +3, but a 1 always fails).

Rival policy (one line): Kestrel moves the console you traded at by 2 against a repeat of your trade at dusk; shown after it lands, as a serial chapter.

Ending shape: final-roll (inspection d6 whose odds the player buys with Morale and surplus Light). Opening shape: free-choice. Rival type: counter. Rival tell: after. Turn band: long (20).
