## Present

```
FERAL RING
The Kepler-442c survey ring is six sealed sectors, native grazers have chewed through the hull, and the Survey Board will hand the ring to whichever tamer holds more sectors when the shuttle lands, or when the ring cracks.

Rules
1. Six sectors in a loop: A Hydroponics (your lab), B Reactor, C Habitat, D Kennels (Kessler's), E Workshop, F Airlock. Each turn: move to an adjacent sector or stay, then take one action there.
2. Tame (1 Serum, roll 4+): harness a feral in your sector. You hold the sector while your beast stands in its pen. One pen per sector.
3. Sabotage (1 Serum, roll 4+): cut the harness on Kessler's beast in your sector. It goes feral and the sector is nobody's until someone tames it.
4. Integrity drops every turn by 1 plus the number of feral beasts loose. Ferals chew the bus.
5. The Bull roams one sector a turn. Taming it costs 2 Serum and a 5+. Its sector counts double and cannot be sabotaged. If it walks into a harnessed beast, roll 1-2 and that harness snaps.
6. Serum: +1 per sector you hold at the start of each turn.
7. Kessler plays by the same rules every turn. You see what she did.
8. Game ends after turn 16 or the moment Integrity hits 0. Most sectors wins; ties go to Kessler.

Goal: Hold more sectors than Kessler when the ring is counted.   Lose if: she holds as many or more, at the shuttle or at the breach.   Turns: 16

Turn 1 of 16 | Integrity 50 | Serum 4 | Held: A (1) | Kessler: D (1) | You: A | Kessler at: D | Bull: C | Feral: B, E
The grazers are through the hull in two places and the Bull, four tonnes of magnetotactic appetite, is pacing the Habitat deck next to the Reactor. Kessler runs her kennel at D on the far side of the ring with four doses of Serum, same as you. Your lab runner holds A. At the current chew rate the ring has twelve turns of hull; the shuttle is sixteen away.
1. Move to B (Reactor) and tame the feral there (1 Serum, 4+).
2. Move to B, no action, bank Serum and wait for the Bull.
3. Move to F (Airlock), empty, the long way round toward E's feral.
4. Stay at A and bank Serum for a Bull attempt.
or say anything else.
```
