## Rulesheet
1. Each turn: dive one site (pay its Charge, draw an encounter, roll 2d6 plus bonuses) or stay home and recharge (+6 Charge).
2. Charge is the sub's battery. You cannot dive a site you cannot pay for.
3. Stores feed and heat the platform. The crew burns 2 Stores every turn. Stores at 0 means evacuation: you lose.
4. Herd: tamed beasts, max 3. Each gives +2 on all rolls at its home site and eats 1 Stores per turn. Release one any time, free.
5. Taming: when a beast is drawn, roll 2d6, 9+ tames it. Failure costs Charge.
6. Winter: the ice closes one site for good on turns 6, 9 and 12, shallowest first. The Lantern Shelf never freezes.
7. A sealed company envelope opens on turn 8.
8. Win: Stores at 12 or more when the icebreaker docks at the end of turn 16.
