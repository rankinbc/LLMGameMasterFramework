# Ash Rule

Premise: Abbot of a cloister above a nuclear-winter bunker network, you write a two-instruction Rule each dawn for a runner who walks the sliding line of bunkers alone, while the larder drains and the Gleaners shadow every lift.

## Rulesheet
1. 6 bunkers stand in a line, each held by one faction, with a pile of goods up to 8. At dusk each pile regrows by a d3 roll minus 1, and the line slides: the head bunker goes to the back.
2. Each day write a Rule, raise a bunker, keep a vigil or call a favour. A Rule is a first and a then instruction that your runner repeats 3 times from the head of the line, alone.
3. Take lifts up to 2 here. Walk steps to the next bunker, stopping at the last. Sweep, unlocked on day 9, lifts up to 1 here and steps on. Lifted goods become stock.
4. Raise: pay 4 stock times the new tier to lift one bunker a tier; the last tier, 3, needs a vigil instead. A raise costs the day's run. A raised bunker regrows 1 more per tier and cannot be camped.
5. Vigil, for the last tier: roll d6 for 4+, one lower for each stock you add, up to 3. A hit pays 4 stock times 3. A miss loses only the added stock, and the day.
6. The Gleaners camp the bunkers you lifted most from, most first: 1 a dusk, 2 from day 9. A camped bunker yields nothing for one day. A day with no lifts leaves no trail.
7. One faction is secretly your ally: its keepers add 1 to every lift that finds goods. A bonus names it, as does getting none from every other faction. From day 11 a known ally can send 10 stock, once, instead of a run.
8. Goal: one bunker at tier 3 within 16 days. You start with 17 stock; the brothers eat 7 each dusk, and stock below zero after that starves the cloister and loses.

## Signature detail
The Rule is a two-instruction loop the runner repeats three times without the Abbot. One of three factions is a secret ally whose keepers add stock to every lift; a bonus reveals it, and so does lifting goods from the other two factions and getting none. The last tier is a vigil: a d6 roll whose odds are bought with extra stock.

## Midpoint plan
Day 9: the sweep instruction unlocks and the Gleaners camp two bunkers a dusk instead of one. Late lever: from day 11 a known ally can send a one-time favour of stock; the vigil for the last tier is the finish (final-roll).

## Rival policy
The Gleaners (counter) camp the bunkers you lifted most from, most first, at dusk (1 a dusk, 2 from day 9), skipping raised bunkers; the tell comes after it lands, and the camp shows as a bracket on the line next dawn.
