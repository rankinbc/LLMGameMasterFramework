# The Bee Tithe

Premise: the sun has dozed off over Orrin Vale, a slow front drifts in, and you are the last Beekeeper. Your bees store sunlight. You carry it between six steadings, paying the tithe that keeps six families calm.

Rolls: FORM=1 SET=60 ROLE=34 LOOP=12 CLOCK=10 TWIST=24 WIN=18 MAP=8 TONE=5 CURRENCY=6
(Trading run / weather system over a farming valley / beekeeper / trade favors, spend reputation / unrest rises when ignored / huge advantage that decays / reach an approval number / single location with 6 districts / mythic / Light)

Sameness brief obeyed: MAP not 9 (rolled 8), primary number is Light (not cash), 2 tracked numbers (Light and Mood/Approval), rival tell hidden/after (strike shown afterward, temper hidden), ending built as cliff (Harvest Count right after the last action), player frame caretaker (beekeeper), decision unit allocate (steading plus amount).

## Rulesheet
1. 6 steadings ring the valley: Orchard, Barley, Mill, Vine, Fen, Hearth. Each turn you walk to one (cost 1 light per step round the ring) and do one thing there. You start at Hearth with 8 light, and you can hold 14.
2. Gather: your bees there make light equal to its sun, but never less than 1. Every steading has a sun from 0 to 4, shown on the status line.
3. Gift 1 to 3 light: that steading's mood rises by gifts x rate. The rate is 1 at sun 3 or more, 3 at sun 0, otherwise 2. Mood tops out at 8.
4. Your blessing adds +4 mood to every gift, losing 2 every 4 turns. From turn 15 (the Golden Hour) every gift counts x2.
5. At dusk, a steading you did not visit loses 1 mood if its sun is 0 or less. Any mood at -3 or lower is a riot: you lose.
6. Each dusk the front cuts the sun of one steading by 2 (two steadings from turn 9) and you see where it fell only afterward. It hunts either the sunniest steading or the one you visited most in the last 3 turns, and you are not told which. Other suns drift.
7. From turn 8, a steading at mood 4 or more will pay a favor, once: its mood falls 2 and you gain 8 light.
8. Every steading starts at mood 1. The Harvest Count comes right after your action on turn 16: total mood (approval) of 36 or more wins, anything less loses.

## Signature detail
The front has a hidden temper (rolled by the seed): it either hunts the sunniest steading or follows the steading you visit most. The player must infer which from where it falls.

## Midpoint plan
Turn 8 favors unlock (reputation can be spent for light). Turn 9 the front falls on two steadings. Blessing fades at turns 5 and 9. Golden Hour doubles gifts on turns 15 and 16. The Harvest Count is checked only after turn 16's action.

## Rival policy
Each dusk the front cuts sun by 2 on the sunniest steading (temper 0) or the most-visited-in-last-3-turns steading (temper 1), two targets from turn 9; ties go to sunniest then ring order.

## Mechanics fingerprint
actions: gather, gift, favor. Tracked: Light, Mood list (Approval total).
