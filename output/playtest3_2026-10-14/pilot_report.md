# Pilot round 3: three games under the hardened harness

Date: 2026-10-14. Three fresh games were generated by following the updated `/play` skill, then played by the same agent. The harness at that point had the universal checks, rule tests, `integrity`, `trace`, `profile`, `ablate` and `autopsy` but not params, look-ahead resampling, or the mutation pass.

## Results

| # | Title | FORM | Result | Fun | Engine bugs found in play | Cosmetic or policy issues | Check rounds | Time |
|---|-------|------|--------|-----|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------------|------|
| 1 | Cistern Docket | triage | Win, 10 of 13 | 7 | 0 | plural text "1 pieces", negative Water shown, heuristic endgame flaw | 4 of 4 | about 75 min |
| 2 | Drawdown Depot | logistics | Loss, 6 beds of 14 | 6 | 0 (one harness bug found in development) | plural text "1 nights", "turn 17 of 16" at the end | 4 of 4 | about 45 min |
| 3 | The Bee Tithe | trading run | Win, 43 of 40 | 6 | 1 (the last action was never offered) | ceiling gifts offered at +0, a 30-option menu | 4 of 4 | about 60 min |

Compared with round 2, where all 10 games had an engine or sheet bug: no game had a crash or an `integrity` entry, one had a real rule-level bug, and two had text defects. The one real bug, an off-by-one in the end-of-budget check, was invisible to every check then in place and was found only by the agent reading a trace.

## What the pilot found, and what was done about it

| Finding | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| `step` ran an action with args that contradicted the offered option, and `t.offered` had the same hole (games 2 and 3) | `_match` rejects args that contradict the option; unit and CLI tests |
| `check_end` fired at the start of the last turn, so the last action was never played (game 3) | `check_end` is tested at the budget turn on sampled states; the trace now says "ended at dusk of turn N; turn N+1 was never played"; the contract and skill state the rule |
| Wrong plurals and a turn counter shown as "17 of 16" (games 1 and 2) | plural lint on all text, a `plural()` helper, turn display capped |
| MENU warning fired when every option had a group; menus of 14 to 30 legal options (games 2 and 3) | menu size is counted after grouping; options may carry `params` (a number range or a set of choices) so amounts and bids are one option |
| Look-ahead too noisy on big menus, so "no better than the heuristic" could be noise (game 2) | two-stage look-ahead with paired rollouts; the warning needs a clear gap |
| Look-ahead, profile and autopsy knew the hidden answer (games 1 and 2) | `resample_hidden` hook; warning when hidden facts exist without it; warning when the heuristic reads hidden state |
| Autopsy trusted a weak heuristic (games 1 and 3) | notes say regrets assume sensible play afterward; a regret on a move that won with a near-zero rating is dropped; a warning when look-ahead beats the heuristic by more than 40 points |
| `biggest_swing` blamed forced moves for event-driven swings (game 1) | the swing reports its cause (choice, or events and dice) and says when the move was forced |
| "Hard turn" counted best versus worst (games 1 and 2) | profile and autopsy report consequential turns and dilemmas separately and skip forced turns |
| Nothing counted forced turns (game 1) | forced-turn share in `check` |
| "3 tracked numbers" not enforced; boards kept off the status line dodged it (games 1, 2 and 3) | `TRACKED` declaration, at most 3, checked against state; `fingerprint` reads tracked_count from it |
| 8 lines of 40 to 60 words (games 1 to 3) | 45-word hard cap per rule line, 36-word warning |
| Rule-test strength unknown (games 1 and 3) | a code-mutation pass: it changes numbers, comparisons and signs in the engine and reports the changes no test or check noticed |
| Tests cannot script `choice`, no way to copy a state (game 1) | `choices=[...]` and `t.copy` |
| GATES and REACH were top-level numbers only (game 1) | `DERIVED` values usable in BOUNDS and REACH |
| A cosmetic edit burned a tuning round; `--free` and `--quick` undocumented (games 2 and 3) | rounds count only when logic or numbers change; the skill documents it |
| `sweep` had no look-ahead; `ablate` took 3 to 11 minutes; `check` took 3 minutes (games 2 and 3) | `sweep --lookahead`; checks run in parallel worker processes: `check` is now under a minute, `ablate` about a minute |
| No way to recover a game after an engine fix (game 3) | `replay` re-runs the saved moves on the saved seed; `replay --apply` adopts the result |
| Step 3 CURRENCY test read played_log, which lacks the field (games 1 and 2) | `sameness.py novelty` and `verbs` read the fingerprints |
| Cannot test the designed trick (game 2) | `trace --do haul,signal:oil=3` scripts the first moves |
| Ending shape and rival tell could not be chosen on purpose (games 1, 2 and 3) | a paragraph in Step 4 with a recipe for each; `sameness brief` names what has been missing |

Not fixed, by design: the wac-claude PreToolUse hook that blocked plain Bash commands as "secret-shaped" (it belongs to another plugin); the Write tool's refusal to let subagents write report files.

## Sameness effect

Adding the three games to the 20 already fingerprinted: `rival_tell` and `ending_shape` dropped out of the stuck list, and `decision_unit` fell from 90 percent pick-an-action to 60 percent. `tracked_count` is still mostly 3, `clock_shape` has a streak of 3 whose CLOCK roll varies (bent), and 90 percent of games are wins.

## Cost

About 45 to 75 minutes and 60 to 85 tool calls per game, against 24 minutes in round 2. Reading and the larger declarations account for most of the rise, plus waiting on slow checks (now fixed). A scaffold that starts every game from a known-good skeleton is the next lever.
