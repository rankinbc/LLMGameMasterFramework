# Drawdown Depot (notes)

Premise: a smuggler in debt runs three night buses for the Low Ward. The water table is dropping, tunnels below the street drain open, the Ward's wells fail, and a bureaucratic Assessor hunts whichever layer is making the most money.

Rolls: FORM=12 SET=55 ROLE=3 LOOP=21 CLOCK=11 TWIST=21 WIN=14 MAP=5 TONE=12 CURRENCY=7

Reading of the rolls
- FORM 12 logistics: buses assigned to layers, with delays (a moved bus pays nothing, impounded buses sit out).
- SET 55 city bus network, ROLE 3 smuggler with a debt (the creditor holds the lien on the Ward).
- LOOP 21 dig deeper: each layer pays more and stalls more; Layer 3 opens as the table falls.
- CLOCK 11 water table drops: tunnels open, wells fail at table 8.
- TWIST 21 wandering threat hunts whoever is richest: the Assessor strikes the layer whose buses paid most.
- WIN 14 keep the settlement (Ward beds) above a threshold until the end. MAP 5 two layers: surface and below. TONE 12 deadpan bureaucratic.
- CURRENCY 7 slots: Beds are the Ward's beds, the number you spend and fight over.

Sameness brief obeyed: MAP not 9 (rolled 5); primary not cash (slots); tracked numbers 2 on purpose (Beds, the bus board; Table is a fixed countdown); rival tell hidden (the Assessor's cycle is inferred from past strikes); ending designed as photo-finish or cliff; player frame manager, not operator; decision unit assign (buses to layers).

Rulesheet
1. You run 3 night buses for the Low Ward, each starting on the Street, Layer 1 or Layer 2 by lot. Each night take one action: move a bus to the Street or an open layer, shore a layer, sink the well, or hold. A bus you move pays nothing tonight.
2. Working buses pay beds overnight: Street +1, Layer 1 +2, Layer 2 +3, Layer 3 +5. Below the Street a bus rolls a d6 and stalls (no pay) on 1 or less on Layer 1, 2 on Layer 2, 3 on Layer 3.
3. The water table starts at 16 and drops 1 each dawn. Layers 1 and 2 are open from the start; Layer 3 opens at table 10 or below.
4. The Ward loses 2 beds a night. At table 8 or below its wells fail and it loses 5 a night instead.
5. After the runs the Assessor works a hidden cycle, repeating in a fixed order you infer from past strikes: 2 nights on the layer whose buses paid most in total tonight, 1 on its haunt (a fixed hidden layer from the Street to Layer 2), 1 at home.
6. A strike impounds one bus there: it pays nothing tonight, stays out 1 more night(s) per the regulations, then returns to the Street. About 8% of nights the Assessor is at recess and does not strike. You learn where it struck only afterward.
7. Purchases: shore a layer once for 1 bed (its stall number drops by 1); sink a well once, after the wells fail, for 6 beds (the wells then cost 2 less a night).
8. The Ward starts with 14 beds and your creditor holds the lien on it: win with at least 14 beds after night 16; lose the moment beds drop below 3.

Signature detail: the Assessor works a hidden cycle (2 richest nights, 1 haunt night, 1 home night, shuffled per seed) plus a hidden haunt layer; every strike names its kind, so the player can reconstruct the cycle. Odd element: recess (8 percent of nights the office is closed).

Midpoint plan: Layer 3 opens at table 10 (night 7); at table 8 (night 9) the wells fail and the loss triples. Late lever: sink a well (6 beds, wells cost 2 less).

Rival policy in one line: each night, by its hidden cycle, strike the layer whose buses paid most in total, strike its haunt, or stay home; a strike impounds the best-paying bus there.
