# Feral Ring - session state

Playtest game 06. Final state (rewritten each turn during play; this is the end-of-game version with the full log).

## Rolls

SET=38 (Monster-infested hinterland with villages) ROLE=30 (Beast tamer) LOOP=14 (Chase a target that moves each turn) CLOCK=5 (Hull or structure integrity decays) TWIST=25 (You can sabotage instead of compete) WIN=6 (Hold the most nodes when the clock ends) MAP=8 (Single location with 6 districts) TONE=4 (Hard sci-fi)

Composition: the hinterland villages became six sealed sectors of a survey ring on Kepler-442c; the monsters are native magnetotactic grazers chewing the ring's superconducting bus; the beast tamer harnesses them with Serum darts; the moving target is the Bull; structure decay is ring Integrity driven by the feral count; sabotage cuts the rival's harnesses; win is most sectors held at the end. Signature detail: the Bull's harness-snap charge, and the rival Kessler who plays by identical rules.

## Rulesheet

1. Six sectors in a loop: A Hydroponics (your lab), B Reactor, C Habitat, D Kennels (Kessler's), E Workshop, F Airlock. Each turn: move to an adjacent sector or stay, then take one action there.
2. Tame (1 Serum, roll 4+): harness a feral in your sector. You hold the sector while your beast stands in its pen. One pen per sector.
3. Sabotage (1 Serum, roll 4+): cut the harness on Kessler's beast in your sector. It goes feral and the sector is nobody's until someone tames it.
4. Integrity drops every turn by 1 plus the number of feral beasts loose. Ferals chew the bus.
5. The Bull roams one sector a turn. Taming it costs 2 Serum and a 5+. Its sector counts double and cannot be sabotaged. If it walks into a harnessed beast, roll 1-2 and that harness snaps.
6. Serum: +1 per sector you hold at the start of each turn.
7. Kessler plays by the same rules every turn. You see what she did.
8. Game ends after turn 16 or the moment Integrity hits 0. Most sectors wins; ties go to Kessler.

Goal: hold more sectors than Kessler when the ring is counted. Lose if she holds as many or more. Turns: 16.

## Hidden facts (now revealed)

- Bull movement: d6 each turn while feral; 1-3 clockwise (A toward B), 4-5 counter-clockwise, 6 stays. Entering a harnessed sector: d6, 1-2 snaps the harness.
- Breach deck: turn 5 new feral at F; turn 9 new feral at C; turn 13 new feral at B. Applied at end of turn, after both tamers act.
- Kessler policy, evaluated after her move, in order: (1) tame a feral in an unheld pen in her sector; (2) tame the Bull if in her sector and Serum >= 2; (3) sabotage a player-held beast in her sector if she is not ahead; (4) otherwise move one step toward the nearest tameable target (feral in an unheld pen, or Bull), ties broken clockwise, then re-evaluate 1-3 in the new sector; (5) if no target exists, move toward the nearest sabotage-able player sector.
- Kessler Serum: start 4, +1 per held sector. Final 12. It never constrained her.
- Turn order: income to both; player moves and acts; Kessler moves and acts; Bull moves; breach deck; Integrity drops by 1 + ferals loose; end check.
- Dice pool pre-rolled (5 per turn, consumed in order: player action, Kessler action, Bull move, Bull snap, spare). Unused slots discarded.
