## Present

```
TRENCH HERD
You run the sub and the animals on Station Kettle, a company mining platform in a polar trench, and the icebreaker that collects the crew does not stop for platforms that have run out of soup.

Rules
1. Each turn: dive one site (pay its Charge, draw an encounter, roll 2d6 plus bonuses) or stay home and recharge (+6 Charge).
2. Charge is the sub's battery. You cannot dive a site you cannot pay for.
3. Stores feed and heat the platform. The crew burns 2 Stores every turn. Stores at 0 means evacuation: you lose.
4. Herd: tamed beasts, max 3. Each gives +2 on all rolls at its home site and eats 1 Stores per turn. Release one any time, free.
5. Taming: when a beast is drawn, roll 2d6, 9+ tames it. Failure costs Charge.
6. Winter: the ice closes one site for good on turns 6, 9 and 12, shallowest first. The Lantern Shelf never freezes.
7. A sealed company envelope opens on turn 8.
8. Win: Stores at 12 or more when the icebreaker docks at the end of turn 16.

Goal: Stores 12+ at end of turn 16.   Lose if: Stores hits 0.   Turns: 16

Turn 1 of 16 | Charge 6 | Stores 14 | Herd: none | Open: Vent 1, Nodule 2, Wreck 3, Lantern 4
Station Kettle hums. Four dive sites, shallow to deep, with their Charge cost:
Vent Garden (1): tube worms, clams, vent crabs. Food.
Nodule Plain (2): manganese nodules the company pays for, mantas, silt.
Wreck of the Halvard (3): a sunk supply ship. Galley stores, battery cells, an octopus that has moved in.
Lantern Shelf (4): deep, rich, and full of lantern eels that bite.
1. Dive Vent Garden (1 Charge)
2. Dive Nodule Plain (2 Charge)
3. Dive Wreck of the Halvard (3 Charge)
4. Dive Lantern Shelf (4 Charge)
5. Recharge (+6 Charge)
or say anything else.
```
