# Sluice Street Audit

Premise: caravan master of the last wagon train into Pyrite Flat. You have one season of mud before the snow closes the pass to lay depots along a creekside street whose pay-dirt slides downstream every night, sell what you learn about the ground to the Land Office, keep Voss Freight off the best lots, and satisfy the Territorial Auditor on the last wagon.

## Rolls

FORM=14 (tile placement) SET=23 (gold rush boomtown) ROLE=8 (caravan master) LOOP=6 (scout then strike, fog of war) CLOCK=8 (winter closes the passes) TWIST=9 (information is the currency) WIN=17 (balance the budget with the reviewer satisfied) MAP=9 (a line of 6 nodes that shifts each turn) TONE=12 (deadpan bureaucratic) CURRENCY=12 (standing) TRACKED=3

Tracked numbers (3): Standing (primary, the CURRENCY roll), the Street board (`lots`: depots, Voss stakes, known ground), Snow (the line of closed lots, which the one plough can move).

## Rulesheet

1. 6 lots run in a line from the pass (lot 1) to the creek (lot 6). Each night you do one thing: scout, build, file, raise or plough. You start with 12 Standing.
2. Every lot lies on Rich, Fair or Poor ground, hidden until scouted. At dusk each open depot of yours pays by its ground (Rich 3, Fair 1, Poor 0), plus 1 for each open depot of yours beside it.
3. After pay, the ground slides one lot toward the creek with what you know; lot 6's ground is lost, lot 1 gets unseen ground. One unannounced night from 9 to 15, a flood slides it twice and washes out lot 6.
4. Scouting a lot shows its ground. Filing your survey pays 5 Standing for each lot whose ground you know, then the Land Office keeps your notes and you know nothing.
5. Build a depot on an empty open lot for 8 Standing, or buy out one of Voss's for 16. From night 8, raising a depot (once each) costs 10, and it then pays 3 more a night.
6. Voss Freight stakes the empty open lot with the richest ground (nearest the creek on a tie) after the dusk slide on night 3 and every 4 nights after. His stake shows you the ground beneath it.
7. From night 8 snow closes lot 1, another lot every 2 nights to lot 3. Closed lots cannot be scouted, built on or raised; their depots pay nothing. Once lot 3 closes, one plough (5 Standing) reopens it for good.
8. After night 16 the Auditor arrives on the last wagon. You win if Standing is at least the Levy of 132 (the books balance) and at least 3 of your depots are open (he is satisfied).

Turns: 16. Goal: at the audit, Standing at least the Levy and at least 3 of your depots open. Lose otherwise.

## Signature detail

The ground (Rich, Fair, Poor) slides one lot toward the creek every dusk, so what you scout is information with a shelf life: it moves with the ground, falls off the creek end, and can be sold to the Land Office (file) at a high price per lot. One unannounced flood from night 9 to 15 slides the ground twice and washes out whatever stands on lot 6, so a reserve of one build cost is the cheap insurance. Voss Freight's stakes show you the ground under them, so his habit leaks information.

## Midpoint plan

Night 8: snow starts closing lots from the pass end (escalation) and the Land Office takes raise petitions (unlock). Snow reaches lot 3 on night 12, which opens the one plough (the late lever, with filing and a rebuild or buyout after the flood). Opening shape is stock-up: scout and file while the first depot slowly pays for the next.

## Rival policy

Voss Freight stakes the empty open lot with the richest ground (ties to the creek end), after the dusk slide, on nights 3, 7, 11 and 15; the tell is "will stake at dusk on night N" the night before, and the stake itself shows the ground beneath it.
